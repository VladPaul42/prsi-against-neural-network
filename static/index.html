    How to play

    • Play one card that matches the suit in play, or the rank of the top card.
    • An Over can be played on almost anything. You then name the suit that is in play. It cannot answer a live 7 or Ace.
    • A 7 makes the next player take 2 cards for every 7 in a row, or play another 7.
    • An Ace makes the next player stand (skip the turn), or play another Ace.
    • You never have to play. You may draw, take the penalty, or stand instead.
    • Playing your last card wins, even if it is a 7 or an Ace.
    • When the draw pile runs out, the discard is shuffled back in, except its top card.
